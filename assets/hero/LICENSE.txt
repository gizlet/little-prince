The Little Prince model (little-prince.glb)

From https://github.com/gizlet/blender-models, exported from
models/little-prince/little-prince.blend with

    blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/build.py -- little-prince --game

which writes dist/little-prince-game.glb.

The model is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The character follows Antoine de Saint-Exupéry's Le Petit Prince (1943).
The book is in the public domain in most countries but not everywhere, and
"Le Petit Prince" is a registered trademark.
