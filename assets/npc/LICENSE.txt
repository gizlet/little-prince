The lamplighter and the fox (lamplighter.glb, fox.glb)

By Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), from Poly Pizza:

- fox.glb: "Fox", https://poly.pizza/m/Bc97C66HKi
- lamplighter.glb: "Worker", https://poly.pizza/m/Yg2bQZO6Hj, recoloured
  into the lamplighter's navy cap and blue coat

Converted with scripts/npc.py (Blender 4.5): colours baked into one palette
texture, the armature's rotation and scale applied, only the animations the
game plays kept.
